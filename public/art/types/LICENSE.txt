home-banner.webp, hero-backdrop.webp, and empty-search.webp (in the parent
directory) are composited from official Pokemon artwork served by
img.pokemondb.net — the same host src/coveo/mapPokemonResult.ts already
treats as the real, live source for every Pokemon sprite this app renders.
Not Nintendo/Pokemon Company brand assets; used here as a demo-only,
non-commercial marketing composite, consistent with how the rest of this
project already uses that host's imagery.
